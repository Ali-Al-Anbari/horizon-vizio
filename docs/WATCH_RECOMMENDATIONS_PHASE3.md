# Horizon Watch recommendations, Phase 3

The Watch homepage renders **Top Picks for You** and, when a suitable verified watch or Like has related candidates, **Because You Watched/Liked [title]**. The current row order is Continue Watching, Top Picks, Because, and Recently Finished; empty rows are omitted. Recommendation cards use the existing portrait poster treatment and open Movie or TV Details. They never start playback or write history. The ranking remains in `universal-search/recommendations.js`; the homepage only paints its results.

## Live Worker validation, 2026-11-05

Four bounded GETs to the existing public Watch preview Worker returned HTTP 200. Cold start returned 40 candidates in 0.212 s; Fight Club + Breaking Bad returned 136 in 0.303 s; invalid movie ID + Breaking Bad still returned 87 in 0.442 s; Interstellar + Dark returned 140 in 0.488 s. The seeded responses included recommendation, similar, discovery, and trending sources. The Fight Club + Breaking Bad sample had 69 movies and 67 shows, 134 posters, 130 backdrops, and genres and vote averages on all 136 candidates. No token or secret field appeared in the response. These observed timings are below the Worker's existing five second deadline; they are a small sample, not a latency guarantee.

With a verified Dark watch and an explicit Interstellar Like, the real Interstellar + Dark response ranked Planet of the Apes (65.36, liked Interstellar), A.I. Artificial Intelligence (48.73, liked Interstellar), Star Trek (33.41, liked Interstellar), FROM (29.72, watched Dark), and Project Hail Mary (18.33, liked Interstellar) among the top picks. The separate Interstellar row selected source-associated Oblivion, The Martian, Star Trek Beyond, Primer, and Ad Astra after reserving Top Picks identities. Scores are deterministic for the same history, feedback, payload, and clock time.

## Row allocation and requests

`selectRows(history, feedback, payload)` uses the Phase 1 ranker for up to eight Top Picks. It excludes verified viewed titles, manually completed titles, both exact rated seed identities, Dislikes, and duplicates. It reserves Top Picks identities before ranking candidate titles with a `recommendation` or `similar` source tied to a real positive seed. The strongest eligible seed is tried first, with a Like stronger than a verified watch. The second row needs at least two candidates with positive diversified scores. A Like-only seed is headed “Because You Liked”; a verified watch is headed “Because You Watched.” Cold start uses the existing trending fallback and omits the second row.

The homepage calls `loadBundle` once per relevant local state change and reads the raw candidate payload from the existing ten minute candidate cache. It does not fetch on focus movement. Concurrent loads are coalesced. Like/Dislike changes rerank the current candidate payload immediately, and the loader fetches additional TMDB candidates in the background when a new positive seed changes the seed set. The loader rechecks feedback after its request, and the homepage ignores out-of-date responses. The Worker remains the only caller of TMDB; the client never receives its token. A six-and-a-half second client abort covers the Worker's five second deadline plus transport margin. A cold failure leaves recommendation rows hidden and the rest of Watch usable. All TMDB metadata remains discovery information; stream availability is resolved only in Details/player flows.

The later carousel refinement lets the single-seed row contain up to twelve source-associated titles with posters and a modest vote/popularity floor. Its same-seed diversity penalty is zero because every item in that row shares the displayed seed; genre and medium diversification still apply. A live South Park seed returned 40 source-associated candidates; the earlier settings showed three positive-score titles after Top Picks reservations, while the refined settings show ten distinct related titles without additional TMDB requests.

## Loopback fixtures and verification

`?mock=1&preview-history=none`, `one-show`, `south-park`, `1`, or `multi-ratings` supply isolated history and ratings. `rec-one-row=1`, `rec-fail=1`, and `rec-delay=1` exercise limited candidates, failure, and delayed responses. Preview history and feedback use in-memory storage on loopback only. Recommendation mock candidates are never written to history.

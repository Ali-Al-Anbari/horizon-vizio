# Watch Phase 3C.1 ranking evaluation

Frozen candidate responses were captured from the deployed Watch recommendation endpoint on 2026-11-12 and sanitized into `tests/fixtures/watch-ranking-phase3c1.json`. They are live TMDB-derived candidates, ranked locally here; this is not a fresh live retrieval. Each comparison uses the same candidate pool and one verified watch of the named seed, one day old, with fixed `now=1800000000000`. Scores are deterministic. Relevance examples are human judgments, not an accuracy metric.

The existing similarity, genre, keyword, and dislike scores are unchanged. Let `R` be their positive relevance sum (similarity + genre + keyword) and `C = min(1, 5 × profile.confidence)`. Quality remains capped at 20, but contributes `quality × [1 − 0.65 × C × (1 − min(1, R / 24))]`. Thus cold start retains full quality; a well-related title also retains it; quality alone loses influence when the profile has evidence. The negative-feedback penalty remains capped at 12. Diversity now caps a repeated-seed charge at one use (default 5), same-type charge at 2, and overlapping-genre charge at 6. The combined penalty is multiplied by `1 − min(0.3, R / 80)`. The existing `seedDiversityWeight: 0` option for the Because row still works.

The 12 pools contain 1,068 candidate records. Final top tens contain zero duplicate canonical identities. Trending-only entries occupied 41 of 60 top-five slots before and zero after; this is source-attribution evidence, not a relevance-accuracy score. Single-seed top tens naturally share the seed genre (9–10 of 10 for the most common genre); a mixed South Park, Severance and Interstellar profile distributes its first ten across all three seeds (3, 3, 4). Generic trending titles remain eligible but no longer outrank clear related candidates through quality alone. Title-level source attribution is retained in the fixture. The Office (US) pool contains a distinct The Office identity, so title strings alone are not used for deduplication.

The benchmark performs 20 rankings per pool in Node 22 on desktop; mean per ranking is 1.39 ms before and 2.19 ms after. These small timings are noisy and do not establish Vizio performance.
